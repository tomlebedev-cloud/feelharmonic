Interview with the performer — “Dive into personality”

A live conversation with the performer — the creator, the artist — straight after the concert, in front of the audience. The aim is to bring the audience closer to the performer. It is a two-way reflection: the performer gets to name their technical, interpretive, artistic and stage experiences, while the listener comes to understand better what an artist on stage is and gets to know the nuances of the world of performance. Having just come off stage, people are open in a way they rarely are otherwise — so things are said here that never make it into an ordinary interview. Filmed and recorded; the recording stays with the organiser.

Length30–45 min

Experiencing transformation through music

The human being — an instrument that creates its own life

Both literally and figuratively.

Each of us carries an instrument within — the body, the breath, the voice, memory. It is played by time, by experience, by the people close to us, even by the silence between them. The only question is this: do we let someone else touch the strings, or do we take up the craft of creating our own sound?

In this space music and art become not entertainment but a way back to yourself — to hear how your inner tone sounds, when it vibrates freely and when it is tense. Just as a musical instrument asks for a relaxed body and, at the same time, conscious attention to every sound, life too invites us to listen closely until it becomes a work of our own.

The path has its own notes

  1. 01 Recognise Hear what sound is resonating in you right now.
  2. 02 Understand Why it sounds that way, and where its tone comes from.
  3. 03 Attune yourself Tune your strings to yourself until the inner dissonance becomes a chord.
  4. 04 Sustain the harmony Once you find it, nurture it every day — like an instrument that needs constant tuning.
  5. 05 Attune others Because a person who sounds in harmony becomes a tuning fork for those around them.

Here, through sound, rhythm and silence, we invite you to pause and feel: what is playing through you today — and what would you like to play yourself?

Personal growth

“Menais mainais” — an exchange through the arts NEW

The arts as a tool for refining behaviour, forming positive habits and enriching the imagination — which lets us build a more beautiful, sustainable, balanced and harmonious relationship with ourselves and with the world around us.

An event where personalities intertwine through different arts — music, word, movement and image — encouraging participants' creative self-expression.

The aim of “Menais mainais” is to bring each participant closer to their inner artist, because “we are all encoded as artists — it's just that not all of us know it…”. It is precisely interactive participation in music and other arts events that can help a person discover their own artistic potential.

About

Music put together as carefully as it is felt

Elena Daunytė is an artist and performer, a prizewinner at international competitions and a member of the family ensemble Regnum Musicale. Her instrument is the cello: she graduated from the M. K. Čiurlionis School of Arts and studied at the Royal Northern College of Music in Manchester and at the Lithuanian Academy of Music and Theatre, in the class of Prof. Rimantas Armonas.

She has played with orchestras in Lithuania and abroad and taken masterclasses with Giovanni Sollima, David Geringas and Frans Helmerson. She has received letters of thanks from Presidents of the Republic of Lithuania Valdas Adamkus and Dalia Grybauskaitė. The music video “Ryto ugnis” was shown in the LRT archive as part of the KLIPVID 2021 competition.

Practical questions

Frequently asked

Do you bring your own sound equipment?
Yes. I have a portable sound system that covers a hall of up to number people and small outdoor events. In acoustic spaces the cello often sounds best with no amplification at all.
Can you take care of the whole event, not just the music?
Yes — I call this an event from A to Z. Programme, performers, stage, sound, catering and photographer are arranged through me, and you get one contact and one invoice. If you only need the artistic part of a wedding or a company celebration, that is just as usual.
I am looking for performers for my celebration. Can you help?
Yes. Booking and liaison is a separate service: I select musicians, dancers, vocalists or actors to suit the occasion, the space and the budget, and coordinate their schedules. All you do is choose from the options prepared for you.
Do you teach?
Yes — one-to-one and in groups, children and adults. Cello or another instrument of your choice, musical understanding, a feel for the stage. Beginners need no previous experience.
How does a therapeutic event differ from a concert?
At a concert you listen; in a therapeutic session you take part. Music, word, scent and movement are arranged for a state rather than an effect, so the group is smaller (8–25 people) and the session is led together with a therapist.
How far in advance should a date be booked?
For the Christmas season — from September, since December weekends fill up first. At other times 4–6 weeks is enough.
Do you issue invoices?
Yes. I work under an individual activity certificate (code DK4429441), so I issue invoices and can sign a service contract — which suits the accounting departments of public-sector institutions.
What does travel outside the city cost?
Travel within 60 km of Vilnius is included in the price. Beyond that a rate of €0.25/km applies, stated in the quote rather than added later.
Can the programme be adapted to our occasion?
Yes, and it usually is. Repertoire, length and the spoken part are agreed before the event. If you have a particular piece in mind, say so early — preparation takes time.
What languages do you work in?
Lithuanian, English and Italian. Concerts, educational sessions and conversations for international guests can be led in English or Italian without an interpreter.
What happens if the event is cancelled?
If cancelled more than 14 days before the event, the deposit is returned or moved to another date. Terms for later cancellation are set out in the contract.
